# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sex.

## Standards

- **Be kind.** Assume good faith; disagreement is fine, demeaning is not.
- **Be professional.** This project records terminals — people paste
  secrets, credentials, and personal data here. Discuss that data only
  with explicit permission, never in issues, PRs, or chat.
- **Critique the code, not the coder.** PRs are reviewed on
  correctness, style, and tests, not on who wrote them.
- **Mind the blast radius.** Report security issues privately (see
  SECURITY.md), not publicly.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to a maintainer or via the security contact in
SECURITY.md. We will review and respond to all reports, take action
proportional to the situation, and follow up as appropriate.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may
be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed
and handled promptly and fairly. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce
the Code of Conduct may face temporary or permanent action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and in the public
when you are explicitly representing the project.
